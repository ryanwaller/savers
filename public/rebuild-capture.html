Browser capture

Save pages, images, links, text, and screenshots as you browse. Captures go to this browser's Savers preview.

Download extension Version 0.2.1 · Chrome

Capture

PageClick the Savers toolbar icon.
Image or linkRight-click it, then choose Save to Savers.
Selected textUse the Save text chip or right-click menu.
CleanShot screenshotCopy it and press ⌘V in Savers, or drag it onto the page.
